Map Size
Tiles keep their positions. Anything outside the new size is removed, and Undo brings it back.
Tile Size
Match the tileset image: 16, 32, 48 and 64 are the usual sizes. Sheets with a thin line between tiles want a gap of 1. Placed tiles stay on their grid cells.
Change Tileset
Tiles already on the map keep their tile numbers, so a tileset laid out differently will change how the map looks.
Send to GameBuilder

This makes a new GameBuilder project with your map as its first level. GameBuilder opens in this tab, so save your map first.

  • Layer 1 becomes the ground the player walks on
  • The other visible layers become decoration in front of it
  • Solid cells decide which ground tiles are walls; with none marked, every ground tile is solid
  • GameBuilder has no flipped tiles, so mirrored tiles arrive the normal way round
Rename Layer
Leave it empty to go back to the plain layer number.
